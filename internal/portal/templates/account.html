Add this relay to Clipp

3 simple steps

Use this discovery URL in each Clipp installation you want to connect.

Open discovery endpoint ↗

  1. 1
    Open relay settings

    In Clipp, open the Peers sidebar, find Relays, and choose Add relay. On a narrow screen, use the menu button to open the sidebar.

  2. 2
    Paste the discovery URL

    Choose Authenticated discovery, paste the URL into HTTPS discovery URL, and save.

  3. 3
    Sign in from Clipp

    Choose Log in for the saved relay, then authorize Clipp using your approved Google account.

{{if .Recorded}}What does Recorded Relay Usage mean?

Confirmed durable usage has a periodic confirmation lag; recent traffic may not yet appear. HOP and STOP endpoints count independently, including twice for the same account; control traffic can count too. This conservative measure is not exact application payload traffic. History retains the current week + twelve preceding weeks as absolute totals, independent of today’s allowance.

{{else}}What does quota committed mean?

Quota committed includes unused funded credit and is not measured traffic. Unused funded credit is lost on restart and never refunded.
