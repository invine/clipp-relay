What does Recorded Relay Usage mean?

Confirmed durable usage has a periodic confirmation lag; recent traffic may not yet appear. HOP and STOP endpoints count independently, including twice for the same account; control traffic can count too. This conservative measure is not exact application payload traffic. History retains the current week + twelve preceding weeks as absolute totals, independent of today’s allowance.

Add this relay to Clipp

Copy the canonical HTTPS discovery URL into each Clipp installation.

Open discovery endpoint ↗

A Portal Session signs you into this website; each Clipp app needs its own authorization. Copying the URL only adds the relay address; it does not connect or authorize an app.{{if not .Active}} Relay use becomes available after your account is approved or restored.{{end}} Opening this URL in an ordinary browser may show an authentication error; that does not mean setup failed.
View setup instructions for Clipp desktop, Android, and Chrome extension
  1. 1
    Open relay settings

    In Clipp, open the Peers sidebar and choose Relays, then Add relay. This setup is available in Clipp desktop, Android, and the Chrome extension.

  2. 2
    Save the discovery address

    Choose Authenticated discovery, paste this address into HTTPS discovery URL, and save.

  3. 3
    Authorize each app

    In each Clipp installation, choose Log in for the saved relay and sign in with the approved Google account.
